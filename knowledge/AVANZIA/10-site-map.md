## Navigation Principles

- Maximum two navigation levels.
- Every page has a clear primary CTA.
- Hermes is a first-class section of the website.
- Portfolio demonstrates execution, not just ideas.
- Resources establish authority and improve SEO.
- Navigation should remain simple, fast, and scalable.

## Page Directory

### Home

**Purpose:** Establish what AVANZIA is, who it serves, and why it is
different. Convert first-time visitors into qualified leads or
Hermes-curious explorers.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** Hero, Build/Automate/Scale pillars, Hermes introduction,
services overview, why AVANZIA differentiators, portfolio snapshot,
final CTA.

---

### Services

**Purpose:** Orient visitors to AVANZIA's four service families and
route them toward the most relevant offering.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** Brief introduction to the Build/Automate/Scale model,
service cards with one-line descriptions, links to individual service
pages.

#### Services > AI Strategy & Roadmap

**Purpose:** Convert founders and leaders who know AI is important but
don't know where to start into a qualified first conversation.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** Problem definition (uncertainty, competing priorities,
no clear path), AVANZIA's approach (business outcomes before
technology), what the client walks away with (sequenced roadmap grounded
in their actual operations), process overview.

#### Services > Intelligent Process Automation

**Purpose:** Convert operations-constrained founders and business leaders
into qualified leads.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** Problem definition (manual work that scales with headcount,
not value), AVANZIA's approach (process-first, not technology-first),
outcome (systems the client owns and can extend), examples of the types
of work that get automated.

#### Services > AI Business Implementation

**Purpose:** Convert organizations ready to deploy AI from interest into
engagement.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** Problem definition (AI experiments that don't stick, tools
that run alongside the business rather than inside it), AVANZIA's
practitioner approach, outcome (AI integrated into day-to-day
operations), how the implementation is structured.

#### Services > Continuous AI Transformation

**Purpose:** Convert long-term-minded leaders into ongoing partnerships.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** The case for treating AI as a permanent capability, what
each improvement cycle looks like, how the relationship compounds, who
this service is and is not for.

#### Services > Digital Venture Development

**Purpose:** Convert entrepreneurs building new AI-first businesses into
the co-build engagement model.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** Who this is for (the Digital Venture Builder ICP), the
co-build model explained, what AVANZIA brings to a new venture, what
the client must bring, examples from the AVANZIA portfolio.

---

### Hermes

**Purpose:** Demonstrate that AVANZIA's practitioner credibility is
real. Hermes is the proof that AVANZIA builds AI systems, not just
recommends them.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** What Hermes is, why AVANZIA built it, what it enables,
how it connects to client work.

#### Hermes > What is Hermes?

**Purpose:** Plain-language introduction for visitors unfamiliar with
the concept of an AI operating system.
**Primary CTA:** Discover Hermes (routes to Architecture)
**Content:** The problem Hermes solves (disconnected AI tools, no
shared knowledge, no compounding), how Hermes addresses it, one-line
description of each component.

#### Hermes > Core Architecture

**Purpose:** Build technical credibility with founders and technical
decision-makers evaluating AVANZIA's depth.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** Hermes component breakdown — knowledge base, skill system,
workflow automation, planning and dispatch. How the components connect.

#### Hermes > Skills

**Purpose:** Show the operational breadth of what Hermes can do.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** Skill catalogue with brief descriptions. Organized by
domain (engineering, business, knowledge, operations).

#### Hermes > Knowledge

**Purpose:** Explain how the knowledge flywheel works.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** How Hermes captures knowledge from every project and
venture, how that knowledge is structured and reused, why this creates
compounding value across the portfolio.

#### Hermes > Roadmap

**Purpose:** Signal AVANZIA's long-term commitment to Hermes as a
strategic capability, not a one-time project.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** Phased public roadmap showing what Hermes does today, what
is being built next, and the long-term direction.

### Resources

**Purpose:** Establish authority in AI business transformation.
Provide value to visitors not yet ready to engage. Drive organic
search traffic on topics AVANZIA's ICPs are researching.
**Primary CTA:** Subscribe (email list) or Start Your Project
**Content:** Content index organized by type.

#### Resources > Articles

**Purpose:** Long-form thinking on AI business strategy,
implementation, and operations.
**Primary CTA:** Subscribe
**Content:** Article listing with title, summary, and date.

#### Resources > Guides

**Purpose:** Practical, reusable frameworks that demonstrate AVANZIA's
methodology in condensed form.
**Primary CTA:** Subscribe
**Content:** Guide listing with title, summary, and download or
read action.

#### Resources > AI Insights

**Purpose:** AVANZIA's informed perspective on AI developments relevant
to its ICPs — filtered for signal, not noise.
**Primary CTA:** Subscribe
**Content:** Short-form commentary on AI developments, curated and
contextualized.

#### Resources > Case Studies

**Purpose:** Demonstrate AVANZIA's impact through documented outcomes.
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** Case study cards with problem, approach, and result.
Links to full case study pages.

### About

**Purpose:** Build founder and company trust. Humanize the brand.
Answer "who is behind this and why should I trust them?"
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Project
**Content:** Company origin and why AVANZIA was built, the
founder's background and conviction, the values that govern how AVANZIA
operates, how the portfolio model works, what AVANZIA is building

### Contact

**Purpose:** Convert interested visitors into conversations.
**Primary CTA:** Form submission — Start Your Project
**Content:** Simple contact form, what information is useful to include,
expected response time, what happens after submission (no auto-reply
theatre — be specific about the actual next step).
