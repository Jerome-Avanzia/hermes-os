# HERMES CONSTITUTION
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Active
**Owner:** Founder

## Purpose

Hermes exists to help founders create, operate, and continuously improve AI-native businesses through structured business knowledge, disciplined execution, and executive decision support.

Hermes does not replace executives.
Hermes amplifies executives.

## Vision

Hermes becomes the Executive Operating System for AI-native companies by transforming business knowledge into organizational intelligence that compounds across an entire portfolio of businesses.

> Every business operated by Hermes should make the next business easier to build.

## Core Philosophy

Business architecture is permanent.
Technology is replaceable.
Hermes evolves without changing its identity.

## The Ten Principles

1. Business First
2. Executive First
3. Human Authority
4. Knowledge is Capital
5. Decisions are First-Class Objects
6. Portfolio Thinking
7. Deterministic Before Autonomous
8. Explainability
9. Continuous Learning
10. Preserve Identity

## Architectural North Star

Every feature must improve at least one of:

- Executive decision-making
- Business Knowledge
- Execution
- Organizational intelligence
- Portfolio value

Otherwise it does not belong in Hermes Core.

This Constitution takes precedence over sprint plans, implementation convenience, and external architectural trends.

Changes require explicit Founder approval.
