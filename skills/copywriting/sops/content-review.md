# Content Review Process

Standard procedure for reviewing marketing copy before publication.

## Scope

This SOP applies to all marketing content produced by the Copywriting capability, including website copy, email campaigns, and social media content.

## Steps

1. **Draft Review** — Verify the copy aligns with the project brief and target audience.
2. **Brand Alignment** — Check consistency with brand guidelines and tone of voice.
3. **Factual Accuracy** — Confirm all claims, statistics, and references are accurate.
4. **Legal Compliance** — Ensure content meets regulatory and legal requirements.
5. **Stakeholder Approval** — Obtain sign-off from the content owner.

## Quality Criteria

- Clear and concise language appropriate for the target audience.
- Consistent brand voice throughout.
- No grammatical or spelling errors.
- All calls-to-action are actionable and measurable.

## Revision Policy

Content requiring more than two revision cycles should be escalated to the content owner for scope clarification.
