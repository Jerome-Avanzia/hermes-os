# Brand Audit Procedure

Standard procedure for conducting periodic brand audits.

## Scope

This SOP applies to scheduled and ad-hoc brand health assessments across all business channels.

## Steps

1. **Gather Materials** — Collect current brand assets, guidelines, and recent communications.
2. **Competitive Analysis** — Review competitor positioning and market trends.
3. **Consistency Check** — Evaluate visual and verbal brand consistency across channels.
4. **Stakeholder Interviews** — Gather internal perspectives on brand perception.
5. **Gap Analysis** — Identify discrepancies between brand intent and market perception.
6. **Recommendations** — Produce actionable recommendations with priority ranking.

## Deliverables

- Brand health scorecard.
- Gap analysis report.
- Prioritized recommendation list.

## Cadence

Brand audits should be conducted quarterly, with ad-hoc audits triggered by significant market changes or brand incidents.
